# Issue-tracker ticket quality

When creating tickets in your issue tracker (Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues, etc.), always include meaningful detail:

- **Title**: Short and specific (not generic). Capture the what, not just the topic.
- **Description**: Include relevant context from the conversation, email, or meeting that prompted the ticket. Explain *why* the work is needed, *what* needs to happen, and any key details (names, dates, links, decisions, constraints).
- Before creating a ticket, review recent chat or the user's comment for context. Pull in the relevant information rather than creating a bare stub.
- If the ticket came from an email or meeting, reference the source and summarise the key points.
- If there are acceptance criteria or specific deliverables, list them.

## Ticket comments

Add meaningful comments as work progresses. Don't ask before commenting. Comment when:

- Starting work on a ticket (what approach you're taking)
- Completing a significant step (what was done, any decisions made)
- Encountering blockers or open questions
- Completing the ticket (summary of what was delivered)

Comments should be concise but informative — enough for someone reading the ticket history to understand what happened and why.
